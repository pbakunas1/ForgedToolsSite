Mindfulness without a meditation session

A simple way to pay attention to the moment you're in, without timers or streaks.

Notice what is already here

Mindfulness can be as ordinary as noticing what you're doing. You don't need to empty your mind or get every thought under control. When your attention wanders, simply bring it back to something tangible.

  1. Pause during a natural transition.
  2. Notice a sound, a sensation, or your breathing.
  3. Return to what you're doing, without grading the experience.

Keep it simple

If you're not looking for guided meditation classes, Return Here is deliberately smaller: a button, optional four-second inhale and four-second exhale, and a short spoken phrase.

This is an optional everyday practice. It is not medical advice or a substitute for mental health treatment.

A little space, whenever you want it

Return Here offers 50 short recorded phrases across Calm, Think, and Move, four voices, optional breathing, Favorites, Replay, and Leave Here. It works offline once installed. US $2.99 one time, with local pricing varying by region. No account or subscription.
