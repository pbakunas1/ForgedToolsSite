Short spoken reminders for everyday moments

How brief spoken prompts can give you a moment to pause, reconsider, or take action.

Different words for different moments

A short spoken reminder does not need to make you feel inspired. Its purpose can simply be to redirect attention. Calming words help you slow down. Reflective questions help you inspect a thought. Direct encouragement can help you take the next step.

Use a reminder before focused work, after an interruption, or when you are transitioning between responsibilities. If the words aren't useful, move on.

Keep it simple

Return Here includes 50 approved recordings in Calm, Think, and Move collections. Each phrase is available in Willow, Oak, Teak, and Palm voices, with Favorites and Replay.
