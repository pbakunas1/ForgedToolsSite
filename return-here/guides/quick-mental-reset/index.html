How to take a quick mental reset

A brief pause can help you return to the next thing without building another routine.

Stop, breathe, return

A reset doesn't require a quiet room or a long session. Stop what you're doing for a moment. Notice your surroundings. Take a comfortable breath, then decide what needs your attention next.

  1. Set down your current task.
  2. Notice your feet on the floor or the sounds around you.
  3. Try a comfortable breath. Four seconds in and four seconds out is an option, not a requirement.
  4. Choose one next action.

Keep it simple

Try this between tasks, before a meeting, or after an interruption. Return Here offers one-button spoken phrases and optional guided breathing. It doesn't require an account or a subscription.

This is an optional everyday practice. It is not medical advice or a substitute for mental health treatment.
