How to pause between tasks without losing momentum

A short transition practice that creates a little space without another productivity system.

Make the transition intentional

Switching tasks can feel abrupt. A short pause marks the end of one responsibility and the beginning of another, without becoming another obligation.

  1. Recognize what you just finished.
  2. Put any genuine follow-up in the task system you already use.
  3. Take one comfortable breath.
  4. Ask what deserves your attention now, then begin.

Don't do this while driving or in another situation where operating a device would be unsafe.

Keep it simple

Return Here can serve as a brief transition cue: tap for one phrase, or hold for optional guided breathing before the words. There are no streaks or sessions to complete.

This is an optional everyday practice. It is not medical advice or a substitute for mental health treatment.

A little space, whenever you want it
