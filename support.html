Return Here: A Moment

Return Here is a simple app built around one interaction: press a button and hear a short spoken phrase.

On iPhone and iPad with iOS 1.1.1, choose Willow, Oak, Teak, or Palm. Press Here for a phrase. To hear the displayed phrase again, press Replay; to hear it in another voice, select that voice before pressing Replay. Save a favorite with the heart and use Favorites to listen to your saved phrases in the selected voice.

The iOS app includes 30 phrases recorded in each of four voices (120 recordings). Audio is included with the app and works offline after installation.

Version 1.1.1 is available on iOS. The matching Android update, including Willow, Oak, Teak, and Palm, has been submitted to Google Play and is pending review.
