Return Here: A Moment

Return Here is a simple app built around one interaction: press a button and hear a short spoken phrase.

Choose Willow, Oak, Teak, or Palm. Press Here for a phrase, use Replay to hear it again, or save a phrase to Favorites. In the expanded edition, press and hold Here for a four-second inhale and four-second exhale before the next phrase. Leave Here provides a place to write and let go of a thought.

The expanded edition has 30 Core, 10 Think, and 10 Move phrases recorded in four voices (200 recordings). Included audio works offline after installation. Features in your installed version may differ until the expanded update reaches your device.

The expanded edition is available on iPhone, iPad, and Android.
